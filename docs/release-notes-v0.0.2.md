> Draft for the v0.0.2 release notes, cut 2026-09-30. Scope: every PR merged

## Runtime & suite

- #240 — upstream suite round-4: **606/679**, the tsx wall dissolved (real
  tsx install + staged child entries), TC39 decorators lowered through the
  real vendored decorators, the fork/exec pre-exec fd fix, and eight
  overgrown files split back under the code-size budget.
- #242 — decision-matrix wave 1: the engine GC leak fixed and the fork
  re-pinned (#239), the node differential leg revived, all 23 staging gaps
  closed, the D9 flip (iOS generated bundles untracked, regenerated by
  `gen.sh`).
- #243 — the **forkpty face** (D-b): honest PTY children serve the node-pty
  terminal path (`ptySpawn` + `pty.event`, proposal v1.9.0 candidate); the
  node-pty shim registers as a cjs-loader builtin face.
- #246 — the **sharp face** (D-c): a pure-JS decoder shim — jpeg-js/pngjs
  vendored and sha256-pinned, hand-written GIF/WebP/SVG/TIFF codecs over
  fflate's zlib; the attachment-local family goes from import-fail to 93/93.
- #247 — terminal-bash local joins the shell-suite platform pin: the W8
  ledger's staged-world pair completes; the real-shell suite runs its six
  tests over the forkpty face.
- #251 — the **socket seam** (D-d): audited loopback-only TCP freezes
  additively as contract **v1.8.0** (`socketListen`/`socketConnect` + the
  `socket` channel, five-rule grant model, audit on every call); all 17
  socket-class TIMEOUT specs re-enter with 0 hangs.

## Known issues

Honest at cut time:

- **Known flakes**: `b4.write.live` (the iOS live-write leg) and
  `win32-dialog` (worker-exit race — ~80% red solo on both sides of #247; a
  sweep green there is retry luck). Neither is a regression of this line.
- **Suite residuals**: 606/679 at #240; 681 specs / 607 green at #247's
  sweep. The named, caused residuals: the ssh×13 family at the TLS wall
  (below), 5 lsp spec failures (small, named shim gaps — #251), and the
  socket-class follow-ups (#251). The full residual ledger at the final
  regression run rides the **regression report PR — not merged at cut time;
  see that PR** for the numbers.
- **Capability-plane device verification awaits hardware**: the camera
  iOS/Harmony real-device legs and the BLE real-device legs are one-click
  scripts that skip loud with no device attached (#252, #254); harmony's
  full parity ran on the CLT emulator, the real-device leg stands by (#253);
  the harmony matrix leg needs a DevEco/hdc target (#250).
- **The socket ssh family's TLS wall**: the 13 ssh specs fail loud at
  `node:tls: createServer is not served` — TLS termination is the socket
  proposal's named non-goal; the next step is the TLS design round, not more
  socket plumbing (#251).
- **v1.9.0 (forkpty) is implemented but not frozen**: the face landed and is
  verified on the darwin CLI, while the contract's frozen base is v1.8.0
  (the socket seam, folded additively on 2026-09-30 by #251) — the forkpty
  face waits beyond that base as a v1.9.0 draft candidate, and the freeze
  follows the owner's process; the v1.6.0 (event channel) and v1.7.0
  (render surface) drafts await the same fold (#243).
